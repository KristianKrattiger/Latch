[p1] The Yongle Emperor was born in Nanjing.

Question: What is the place of birth of Empress Xu's husband?
Expected answer type: entity

(This question needs zero operator calls -- it's a pure two-hop bridge retrieval:
find "husband" entity, then find that entity's birthplace. Per OPERATORS.md, bridge

references. Emitting an empty array here is CORRECT, not a failure -- it tells the
scoring harness "no operator needed, this is pure retrieval," which is a distinct,
trackable category, not the same as "couldn't formalize it.")
